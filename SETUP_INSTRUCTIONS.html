🚀 Vercel Environment Variables - Just Copy & Paste!

Go to your Vercel dashboard: Click here

Then click "Add" and copy-paste each one:

DATABASE_URL
NODE_ENV
JWT_SECRET
SESSION_SECRET
ADMIN_USERNAME
ADMIN_PASSWORD
ADMIN_EMAIL

After adding all 7:

  1. Go to Deployments tab
  2. Click three dots (•••) on latest deployment
  3. Click Redeploy
  4. Wait 2 minutes
  5. Visit: https://patrimoine-jet.vercel.app
